## 🚩 The Problem: India's Justice Gap & Language Barrier

- **80%+ Indian Citizens Cannot Understand Legal English**: Contracts in India—such as 11-month residential rental agreements, PG terms, delivery partner gig agreements, 2-year IT training bonds, instant app loans, and police notices—are drafted exclusively in dense English legalese.
- **Predatory Terms Signed Blindly**: Citizens routinely sign away rights, accept unilateral deposit forfeitures, mandatory non-compete clauses (which are legally void under Section 27 of the Indian Contract Act), and unlimited liability without knowing what they signed.
- **Prohibitive Consultation Costs**: A single consultation with a private advocate costs **₹5,000 to ₹50,000+**, completely unaffordable for gig workers, students, domestic help, and tier-2/3 families.
- **The Unused Constitutional Right (Article 39A)**: While Article 39A guarantees **100% free legal aid** via NALSA and State Legal Services Authorities to women, workers, and individuals earning under ₹3 Lakhs/year, over 90% of the eligible population does not know how or where to access it.

## ⚡ Core Features Aligned to the Problem

### 1. Multilingual Simplification (सरल हिंदी + Plain English)
- **Input**: Any Indian legal document or pasted text.
- **Output**: Side-by-side **सरल हिंदी** (written for an 8th-grade education level in Devanagari script) and **Plain English**.
- **Voice Readout**: In-browser speech synthesis for Hindi (`hi-IN`) and Indian English (`en-IN`) with playback speed controls.

### 2. Visual Risk Score Meter (0–100 Predatory Gauge)
- **0–30 Green (Safe / Fair)** | **31–70 Amber (Moderate Caution)** | **71–100 Red (Predatory / Trap)**.
- **Predatory Clause Audit**: Identifies exact quotes from the contract, provides a clear explanation in Hindi and English of why the clause is dangerous, and generates a **fair counter-clause** you can ask the counterparty to accept.

### 3. Rights & Obligations (Power Imbalance Detector)
- Compares **"What are MY Obligations?" (मेरी ज़िम्मेदारियाँ)** side-by-side with **"What are THEIR Obligations?" (उनकी ज़िम्मेदारियाँ)**.
- Highlights unilateral termination clauses, arbitrary fine powers, and liability waivers.

### 4. Voice Consultation & Q&A (बोलकर पूछें)
- **Voice-In**: Press the microphone button and speak in Hindi or English (e.g. *"क्या 2 साल का सर्विस बॉन्ड भारत में कानूनी है?"*).
- **Voice-Out**: Real-time contextual legal information referencing the **Indian Contract Act 1872 (Section 27)**, **Model Tenancy Act**, and **Consumer Protection Act 2019**, spoken back automatically.

### 5. Article 39A Free Legal Aid Directory
- **National Helplines**: 
  - 📞 **NALSA Toll-Free Helpline: `15100`** (National Legal Aid)
  - 📞 **Tele-Law (Ministry of Law & Justice): `14431`**
  - 📞 **NCW Women Helpline: `7827170170` / `1091`**
  - 📞 **National Consumer Helpline: `1915`**
  - 📞 **Labour & Gig Helpline (Shram Suvidha): `14434`**
  - 📞 **Cyber Crime Helpline: `1930`**
- **State Directory**: Complete database of State Legal Services Authorities (SLSA) and District Legal Services Authorities (DLSA) across all **28 States and 8 Union Territories** with physical office addresses, phone numbers, and direct dial links.

### 6. Regional Pre-Signing Action Checklist
- Customized 5-step checklist with interactive checkboxes that persist across sessions.
- Red-flag warning list and one-click **PDF Export** and **WhatsApp Summary Sharing**.

### 7. Built-in Hindi Legal Glossary (कानूनी शब्दकोश)
- Searchable directory of 15+ intimidating legal terms (Indemnity, Lock-in, Forfeiture, Non-compete, Liquidated Damages, As-is-where-is, Jurisdiction, Arbitration) translated into vernacular Hindi.

## 🚀 Running Locally

1. **Clone the repository**:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/arjunpv1312/legal-assistant-antigravity.git
   cd legal-assistant-antigravity
   ```

2. **Configure API Key**:
   Create a `.env` file in the root directory:
   ```env
   GEMINI_API_KEY=your_gemini_api_key_here
   PORT=3000
   ```

3. **Start the server**:
   ```bash
   npm start
   ```

4. **Open in Browser**:
   Navigate to `http://localhost:3000`.

---

## 🌐 Deploy to Vercel

The repository contains `vercel.json` and a serverless API proxy at `api/gemini.js`:
1. Push to GitHub.
2. Import repository in [Vercel](https://vercel.com).
3. Add environment variable `GEMINI_API_KEY`.
4. Deploy!

## 📜 Legal Disclaimer

*LegalBridge is an AI-powered legal document literacy assistant built to bridge India's justice divide. It does not provide formal legal representation or create an attorney-client relationship. Under Article 39A of the Constitution of India, low-income citizens, women, workers, and undertrials can obtain free legal representation by dialing **15100 (NALSA)** or visiting their local District Legal Services Authority (DLSA).*
