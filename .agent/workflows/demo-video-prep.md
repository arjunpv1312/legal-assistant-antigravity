## 🎙️ Part 2: 90-Second Demo Pitch Script

> *"Every day, individuals, freelancers, and small business owners sign contracts packed with dangerous liabilities, uncapped indemnities, and unfair terms. Legal counsel costs upwards of $500 an hour, and plain English is rare. Meet **Legal Assistant Pro**."*

### ⚡ Product Showcase (0:12 – 1:12) — The 5 AI Tools

* **First-Load Security & Document Simplifier (0:12 – 0:25)**
  > *(Visual: Show API Key modal validating -> Click '📂 Sample Contracts' -> 'Mutual NDA' -> Press 'Ctrl+Enter' -> Shimmer Skeleton -> Plain English output)*  
  > *"Users connect securely using their own Google Gemini API key stored strictly in their browser. First, our **Document Simplifier** takes dense 20-page legalese and translates it into crystal-clear plain English rights, obligations, and financial terms."*

* **Risk Analysis & Interactive Clause Heatmap (0:25 – 0:40)**

  > *"Next, our **Risk Audit & Interactive Clause Heatmap** scores the contract and highlights liability traps in color. Click any flagged clause to inspect why it's hazardous, view financial exposure, and copy a **safer counter-proposal** in one click."*

* **Contract Comparator (0:40 – 0:52)**
  > *(Visual: Click 'Contract Comparator' -> Click '🔍 Compare & Highlight Variances')*  
  > *"Reviewing a counter-offer? The **Contract Comparator** performs side-by-side variance analysis with a structured discrepancy matrix showing exactly who benefits from each revision."*

* **PDF Ingestion, Dark Mode & Resilience (1:04 – 1:12)**
  > *(Visual: Drag & drop PDF into dropzone -> Toggle Dark Mode 🌙)*  
  > *"Drag-and-drop PDF ingestion, sleek dark mode, and automated 429 rate limit recovery—all in a single, blazing-fast web application."*

## 🎬 Part 3: Video Recording & Editing Checklist

| Stage | Tool / Setting | Recommended Configuration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Screen Capture** | OBS Studio / ScreenFlow | **Resolution:** `1920x1080` (1080p)<br>**Framerate:** `30fps` or `60fps`<br>**Window:** Clean browser window (hide bookmarks bar, dark system theme). |
| **Audio Recording** | Audacity / Rode / Shure | Record clear, confident voiceover. Apply gentle noise reduction and compressor to normalize levels to -14 LUFS. |
| **Pacing & Speed-up** | CapCut / Premiere / DaVinci | Speed up contract pasting and AI loading spinners by **`2x – 4x`** so the video never pauses or buffers during evaluation. |
| **Captions & Callouts** | Auto-captions | Bold white text on semi-transparent dark background for critical feature tags (*"🔴 Inline Clause Heatmap"*, *"📥 One-Click PDF Export"*, *"🌙 Dark Mode"*). |
